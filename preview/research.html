01 / Rainfall

Tropical Cyclone Rainfall

How accurately do climate models capture rainfall associated with tropical cyclones? My work examines rainfall distribution, frequency, and storm-centered spatial structure, using observations and numerical simulations to characterize model errors.

02 / Prediction

Seasonal Prediction and Cyclogenesis

I explore how large-scale environmental conditions relate to tropical cyclone activity and seasonal rainfall. This includes evaluating subseasonal-to-seasonal hindcasts and examining atmospheric conditions associated with cyclone formation.

03 / Modeling

Regional Modeling and Bias Correction

With regional weather and climate models, I investigate the influence of large-scale boundary forcing on the simulation of tropical cyclone rainfall, including the potential of boundary-condition bias correction and dynamical downscaling.
